The toolkit / Eight mental models

The semester in one picture

01 · Risk–Return Asymmetry Map

Weigh the upside against financial, career, family and mental risks. Ask who gains, who bears the loss, and whether they can survive it.

02 · Legal × Ethical Grid

Check legality and ethics separately. Permission to act does not settle whether the people affected are treated fairly.

03 · CEAI Chain

Turn an external opportunity into action through management support, discretion, rewards, time and workable boundaries. Compare progress with competitors and your own history.

04 · Mission–Margin Axis

Ask whether sales fund the mission and the mission strengthens the business. Test whether that relationship survives a bad year.

05 · Diverge–Converge Loop

Define the problem, generate alternatives, then run a cheap test that could disprove the idea. Use what you learn to redefine the problem.

06 · Decision Pattern Grid

Match decision effort to reversibility and downside. Move quickly on small, reversible choices; investigate and stage major commitments.

07 · Acquisition Allocation + Integration Ladder

Identify the value you are buying, then choose absorb, symbiosis or preserve. Protect the people and routines that create that value.

08 · Franchise Dial

Trade control for capital and speed only when the unit is codifiable, profitable and monitorable. Your reputation remains at risk.

CEAI: benchmark against competitors and against the company’s own history. Ask students which model would change a decision they face now. The acquisition allocation is evidence about the sources of value, not a substitute for investigating the people and routines that produce it.

Apply / From intention to growth

A roadmap to your entrepreneurial journey

  1. Know your stakes

    Define the return you want and the losses you can bear.

    Risk–Return Asymmetry

  2. Set your boundaries

    Choose whom you serve, what you stand for and which lines you will not cross.

    Legal × Ethical · Mission–Margin

  3. Create room to build

    Secure support, autonomy, time and a sponsor who can protect the work.

    CEAI Chain

  4. Test the opportunity

    Generate alternatives. Run the smallest useful test and revise from evidence.

    Diverge–Converge

  5. Commit deliberately

    Check the downside and what you can undo before increasing your exposure.

    Decision Pattern Grid

  6. Choose how to grow

    Build, acquire or license. Preserve what creates value and prove what you copy.

    Integration Ladder · Franchise Dial

At every step: observe → decide → act → learn → adapt.

This is a route to revisit, not a fixed sequence. New evidence can send you back to your assumptions, boundaries or choice of growth model.

The habit to carry forward

Make the next decision better

Make the assumptions visible.
Match the commitment to the evidence.
Change course when you learn.

Your decisions are connected. Ask what each choice makes possible — and what it makes harder to undo.
