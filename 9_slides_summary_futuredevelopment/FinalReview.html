The semester in one picture

Observe→ Decide→ Act→ Learn→ Adapt

Adapt is the step people skip. Skipping it is the difference between ten years of experience and one year of experience repeated ten times.

Walk each step out loud. Observe: you never get the full picture. Decide: at some point reading stops and you commit. Act: it touches the world. Learn: the world answers, usually not how you predicted. Adapt: you change the next decision.

What the simulations were for

The simulation was never about getting the “right” answer.

It was about making a decision, seeing the consequence, and improving the next decision.

That is exactly what the reflections asked for: actions taken, lessons learned, improvements for next time.

Thirty percent of your grade was you practicing Learn and Adapt in writing.
