Team Formation Criteria

  • Aim for teams of 4-5 students.
  • Balance interests, skills, schedules, and communication styles.
  • Identify early roles: coordinator, research lead, customer discovery lead, prototype or website lead, and presentation lead.

Resource Introduction

  • HuskyCT for announcements, grades, and assignment submission.
  • UConn library resources for case studies, company data, and citations.
  • Writing Center support for essays, reflections, and project communication.
  • Course simulation platform for in-class decision exercises.

Personal Project Portfolio

Each student will build a personal project portfolio in OneDrive.

The portfolio should show your entrepreneurial learning, career direction, and five-year growth plan.

First Team Task

  • Exchange contact information.
  • Set recurring work time.
  • Choose a shared workspace.
  • Draft team norms for communication, deadlines, and conflict resolution.
