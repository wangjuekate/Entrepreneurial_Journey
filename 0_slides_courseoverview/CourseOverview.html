MENT 2234: The Entrepreneurial Journey

What This Course Is About

Entrepreneurship is the work of creating and growing new ventures under uncertainty.

We will examine business models, social foundations, creativity, resource acquisition, and the management skills entrepreneurs need.

Three Course Areas

  • Social foundations of entrepreneurship: networks, culture, access, and opportunity.
  • Business model development: designing, testing, and revising venture ideas.
  • Critical entrepreneurial management skills: decisions, resources, validation, and growth.

Learning Objectives

  • Analyze how social structures shape entrepreneurial opportunity.
  • Develop and evaluate viable business models.
  • Apply opportunity recognition, resource acquisition, and validation skills.
  • Generate creative solutions through design thinking and problem solving.
  • Make strategic decisions under uncertainty and resource constraints.

Semester Rhythm

Most topic weeks follow a Tuesday concept session and a Thursday simulation reflection.

Team presentations occur in Week 7 and Week 15. The final week focuses on website submission and review.

Assessment Snapshot

  • Individual assignments: 60%.
  • Team new venture development project: 40%.
  • Major deliverables: team formation, midterm website review, final presentation, and final website submission.

Participation Expectations

  • Prepare before class through blog posts, comments, readings, and case notes.
  • Engage in simulations as a decision maker, not a spectator.
  • Use evidence when defending recommendations.
  • Give constructive feedback to classmates.
