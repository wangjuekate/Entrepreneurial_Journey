Week 8: Tuesday, October 20

Creativity as Entrepreneurial Skill

Creative thinking is the ability to generate, combine, test, and communicate new possibilities.

In entrepreneurship, creativity is useful when it helps solve a real problem for a real user.

Divergent and Convergent Thinking

Divergent

Generate many options.

Delay judgment.

Look across domains.

Ask unusual questions.

Convergent

Select promising options.

Test assumptions.

Fit ideas to constraints.

Choose what to build next.

Design Thinking Loop

  • Empathize with users.
  • Define the problem.
  • Ideate possible solutions.
  • Prototype quickly.
  • Test and revise.

Netflix Preview

Netflix shows creative thinking across multiple business model changes: DVD rentals, streaming, original content, and global distribution.

The case asks how a venture keeps reinventing itself without losing strategic focus.
