Stage 1 — Key takeaways

Diagnose the model before growing it

Common Pitfalls & Successes: Distinguish demand, cash, and sustainable economics.

  • Combine the four perspectives. The CFO holds the cash ceiling, the CMO the pricing choices, the COO the ticket-deal requirements, and the CTO the data capabilities.
  • Do not confuse popularity with viability. Subscriber growth does not establish that revenue covers ticket costs. Check the economics before funding more acquisition.
  • Use the decision grid. Ask how costly a mistake would be and how easily it could be reversed. Annual prepay brings in cash but locks the price for 12 months.

Stage 2 — Key takeaways

Change the economics within the runway

Common Pitfalls & Successes: Measure first; keep the cost of changing course manageable.

  • Coordinate the whole allocation. Cost visibility (15) + ticket renegotiation (10) + a cost-covering price with a usage cap (5) use all 30 planning units.
  • Build the evidence the decision needs. The COO needs per-subscriber attendance and cost data to negotiate; the CMO needs it to set pricing. In this scenario, off-the-shelf analytics lacks card-level cost.
  • Watch the commitments behind the cash. Annual prepay preserves the $9.95 obligation. Expected financing is not available funding, and cutting overhead alone leaves ticket economics unchanged.

Stage 3 — Key takeaways

Prioritize survival and a viable model

Common Pitfalls & Successes: Persist, pivot, or stop using evidence about the cost of continuing.

  • Put Money and Ticket costs first. Available resources bound the response; changing the input cost addresses the model’s immediate economic problem.
  • Place Tech before Growth. Measurement enables negotiation and pricing. Growth comes last in the template because expanding a loss-making offer adds to the cash problem.
  • Set a review point before expanding again. Examine subscriber revenue, ticket cost, usage, retention, and cash. State what evidence would support continuing, revising, or stopping the offer.
