Acquiring Enterprises

Week 10: Tuesday, November 3

Why Companies Acquire

Acquisitions can help firms access talent, technology, intellectual property, customers, brands, or new capabilities.

The challenge is not only buying a company, but preserving the value that made it worth buying.

Acquisition Motives

  • Capability acquisition: get skills or technology faster than building internally.
  • Market access: reach customers, channels, or geographies.
  • Strategic renewal: enter a new growth path.
  • Defensive move: prevent competitors from gaining an asset.

Integration Risks

  • Destroying culture or creative autonomy.
  • Overpaying because of excitement or fear.
  • Losing key employees after acquisition.
  • Forcing the acquired company into incompatible processes.
  • Failing to define post-acquisition decision rights.

Disney and Pixar Preview

Disney's acquisition of Pixar is a case about buying creative capability while protecting the culture that produced it.

The question is how to integrate without erasing the acquired firm's advantage.

Discussion

  • What should an acquirer preserve?
  • What should an acquirer integrate?
  • How do leaders know whether an acquisition is worth the price?
