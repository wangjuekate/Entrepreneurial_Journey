Simulation Reflection: Disney's Acquisition of Pixar

Week 10: Thursday, November 5

Case Setup

Disney needed renewed animation strength, while Pixar had a powerful creative process and successful films.

Your team will decide how Disney should structure the acquisition and integration.

Decision 1: Deal Logic

  • A: Treat Pixar mainly as an asset Disney can absorb.
  • B: Treat Pixar as a creative capability that must retain autonomy.

Decision 2: Culture

  • A: Standardize Pixar under Disney processes.
  • B: Preserve Pixar's creative process and use it to strengthen Disney animation.

Decision 3: Leadership

  • A: Replace Pixar leadership with Disney executives.
  • B: Give Pixar leaders authority over animation decisions.

Reflection Questions

  • What value was Disney really buying?
  • Which integration choice protected that value?
  • How can an acquisition create value for both sides?

Takeaway

Successful acquisitions protect the source of value before forcing integration.
