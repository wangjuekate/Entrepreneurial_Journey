

## Grading and assignment reference

| Component | Course weight |
|---|---:|

| Final report and video | 20% |

Blogs 10%; simulation reflections 40%; participation 10%; midterm report and video together 20%; final report and video together 20%. Team registration is required setup with no separate course weight. Preserve the existing Blackboard allocation between each report and video; use the video scoring criteria already posted there. Report rubric points (midterm 60 plus up to 4 bonus; final 50) are not course percentages. All existing Blackboard deadlines and cutoff times remain unchanged.

Students work in five-person teams to analyze one selected company across the midterm and final. Weekly simulations use four functional leads (Financial, Marketing, Operation, and Tech) plus an Account Manager. The introductory exercise uses a smaller role set.

Use the [midterm guide](Mid-term.md), [final guide](Final.md), and [report rubric overview](Term_Project_Rubric_Startup.md) for project requirements. The existing weekly reflection length (1,000–1,500 words) and blog length (50–100 words) remain in effect.

# Simulation Reflection

# List of projects for Midterm and Final



# Midterm Presentation Video

Submit your team pitch video for the midterm that cover the following points: 



A. Sweets and Bitterness of Entrepreneurs 

1. Describe potential ups and downs for the chosen startup

2. Comment on the narratives and journey of the startup

3. Analyze the trade-offs entrepreneurs face



B. Ethical and Legal Standards 

1. Identify the ethical and legal standards relevant to the chosen startup

2. Evaluate whether the startup is following these standards

3. Recommend areas for improvement



C. Corporate Entrepreneurship Analysis 

1. Assess intrapreneurial activities within the organization

2. Evaluate resource allocation for internal ventures

3. Analyze organizational support for innovation



D. Social Entrepreneurship Impact 

1. Identify social or environmental problems addressed

2. Evaluate the balance between social mission and financial sustainability

3. Assess impact measurement approaches








# Final Pitch Video

Record and submit a video that covering the following points based on the company that your team selected for final project.



A. Business Idea Presentation

1. Concisely introduce the business concept

2. Focus on clarity and compelling initial description



B. Creative Thinking Demonstration

1. Present innovative problem-solving approaches

2. Demonstrate opportunity recognition and reframing

3. Show resource recombination strategies



C. Common Pitfalls and Success Factors

1. Identify potential pitfalls for the venture

2. Explain mitigation strategies

3. Articulate success factors and how they apply



D. Acquisition, Franchising, and Partnership Strategy

1. Evaluate acquisition vs. organic growth options

2. Assess franchising potential if applicable

3. Identify collaboration partners and explain rationale

4. Discuss venture capital readiness and business plan alignment
