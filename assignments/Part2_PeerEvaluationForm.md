### Evaluation Criteria

- **Task completion:** Completed assigned work by agreed-upon deadlines.
- **Quality of work:** Produced accurate, thoughtful work that met project requirements.
- **Communication:** Responded to messages and kept the team informed about progress or obstacles.
- **Collaboration:** Participated constructively, listened to others, and supported team decisions.
- **Accountability:** Took responsibility for commitments and addressed problems when they arose.
