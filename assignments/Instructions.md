# Peer Evaluation
If a team member is not actively participating in the group project, the team leader may take over their tasks in SkillSimm. When submitting the report, team leaders may attach a peer evaluation request (Part 1) at the end of the report. After receiving the request, I will initiate a peer evaluation and ask each team member to complete Part 2. Individual grades will then be assigned based on each member’s contributions.

If a team leader is not actively engaged, team members can reflect to me and I will switch the leader.
