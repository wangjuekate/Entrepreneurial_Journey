Part 1: Request for Peer Evaluation

Use this section to request an instructor review of concerns about a team member’s contribution. Describe specific actions, assignments, and dates rather than making assumptions about the person’s intentions.

Team name or number: ____________________________________

Project or assignment: ____________________________________

Name(s) of student(s) submitting this request: ____________________________________

Date: ____________________________________

Name(s) of team member(s) whose contributions are a concern: ____________________________________

1. What work was the team member expected to complete, and by when?

Include the agreed-upon responsibilities and deadlines.

2. What concerns have you observed?

Provide specific examples, such as missed deadlines, incomplete work, missed meetings, or unanswered messages. Note any work the member has completed.

3. How has this affected the team or project?

4. What steps has the team taken to address the concern?

Describe attempts to communicate, clarify expectations, or redistribute work. Include dates and the team member’s response, if any.

5. What assistance are you requesting from the instructor?

Supporting documentation, if available: List relevant task records, document revision history, or communications. Include only information relevant to the concern.
