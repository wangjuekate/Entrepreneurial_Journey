Corporate Entrepreneurs

Week 4: Tuesday, September 22

What Is Corporate Entrepreneurship?

Corporate entrepreneurs create new products, services, markets, or business models inside existing organizations.

They use the resources of a large firm while fighting the inertia of that same firm.

Advantages and Constraints

Advantages

Access to capital, talent, customers, infrastructure, and brand.

Ability to scale successful ideas quickly.

Constraints

Bureaucracy, legacy incentives, internal politics, and risk aversion.

Pressure to protect existing revenue streams.

Skills Needed

  • Frame opportunities in language the organization understands.
  • Build internal coalitions across functions.
  • Experiment without threatening the core business too early.
  • Use metrics that show learning and strategic fit.

AWS Preview

Amazon Web Services illustrates how an internal capability can become a major external business.

The case asks how a company recognizes that an internal tool can become a platform for other firms.

Discussion

  • Why might a large company ignore a promising internal opportunity?
  • What makes corporate entrepreneurship different from startup entrepreneurship?
  • How should leaders protect a new idea before it proves itself?
