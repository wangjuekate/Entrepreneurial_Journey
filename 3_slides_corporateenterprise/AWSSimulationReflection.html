Simulation Reflection: Amazon Web Services

Week 4: Thursday, September 24

Case Setup

Amazon developed infrastructure capabilities to support its own operations.

The strategic question is whether those capabilities should remain internal tools or become external services for developers and businesses.

Decision 1: Market Entry

  • A: Keep infrastructure internal to protect operational advantage.
  • B: Offer infrastructure services externally and build a new business line.

Decision 2: Resource Allocation

  • A: Fund only projects tied directly to the retail core.
  • B: Fund a separate team with room to build cloud services.

Decision 3: Internal Buy-In

  • A: Sell the idea as a technical side project.
  • B: Frame it as a strategic platform with customer and revenue potential.

Reflection Questions

  • What evidence would convince leadership to support AWS?
  • What internal resistance would you expect?
  • How can a corporate entrepreneur protect experimentation time?

Takeaway

Corporate entrepreneurship turns internal capabilities into new sources of value when leaders give ideas structure, evidence, and strategic protection.
