Simulation Reflection: McDonald's

Week 11: Thursday, November 12

Case Setup

McDonald's scaled through franchising by combining brand, operating systems, training, real estate, and consistent customer experience.

Your team will decide how to protect quality while expanding through franchisees.

Decision 1: Standardization

  • A: Allow franchisees broad flexibility to fit local markets.
  • B: Standardize core operations while allowing limited local adaptation.

Decision 2: Franchisee Selection

  • A: Prioritize operators with the most capital.
  • B: Prioritize operators who fit the system and can execute standards.

Decision 3: Growth Pace

  • A: Expand quickly to claim territory.
  • B: Expand at the pace training, monitoring, and supply systems can support.

Reflection Questions

  • Which decisions protect the brand?
  • What should franchisees control?
  • What does the franchisor owe franchisees?

Takeaway

Franchising scales a business only when the system is clear, teachable, profitable, and enforceable.
