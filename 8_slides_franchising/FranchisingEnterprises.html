Week 11: Tuesday, November 10

What Is Franchising?

Franchising is a growth model where a franchisor grants independent operators the right to use a brand, system, and operating model.

It allows expansion through local ownership while requiring strong standards and support.

Why Franchise?

Benefits

Faster geographic growth.

Local operator motivation.

Shared investment burden.

Repeatable operating model.

Risks

Quality inconsistency.

Brand damage.

Franchisee conflict.

Weak training or monitoring.

Franchise Readiness

  • A proven, repeatable model.
  • Clear unit economics.
  • Documented processes.
  • Training and support systems.
  • Brand standards and monitoring.

McDonald's Preview

McDonald's illustrates the power of standardized systems, real estate strategy, operations discipline, and franchisee alignment.

The case asks how a company scales consistency while relying on local operators.

Discussion

  • What should be locally adapted?
