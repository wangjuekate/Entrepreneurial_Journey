Simulation Reflection: TOMS Shoes

Week 5: Thursday, October 1

Case Setup

TOMS built a recognizable brand around giving shoes to children in need.

Your team will decide how to balance simple messaging, measurable impact, and long-term sustainability.

Decision 1: Impact Model

  • A: Continue one-for-one donations because the model is easy to understand.
  • B: Shift toward local partnerships, community needs, and measurable outcomes.

Decision 2: Brand Message

  • A: Emphasize the emotional story of giving.
  • B: Emphasize transparent evidence about impact and limits.

Decision 3: Growth

  • A: Expand product categories quickly to grow donations.
  • B: Grow more slowly while testing whether each product supports the mission.

Reflection Questions

  • What impact claims are strongest?
  • What impact claims need more evidence?
  • How can a social enterprise avoid mission drift?

Takeaway

Social entrepreneurship requires a model that is emotionally compelling, financially durable, and empirically honest.
