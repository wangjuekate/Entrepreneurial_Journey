Social Entrepreneurs

Week 5: Tuesday, September 29

What Is Social Entrepreneurship?

Social entrepreneurs build ventures that pursue social impact through entrepreneurial action.

They must design a model that creates value for beneficiaries, customers, partners, and the organization itself.

The Dual Mission

Impact

Who benefits?

What problem is reduced?

How is improvement measured?

Sustainability

Who pays?

What resources are required?

Can the model keep operating?

Common Tensions

  • Mission drift when revenue becomes dominant.
  • Weak financial model when impact is not connected to payment.
  • Overclaiming impact without evidence.
  • Dependency on donations, partners, or publicity.

TOMS Shoes Preview

TOMS popularized the one-for-one model by donating shoes for purchases.

The case helps us evaluate whether a simple impact story produces durable social value.

Discussion

  • What makes a social enterprise different from charity?

  • Can a powerful story hide weaknesses in the model?
